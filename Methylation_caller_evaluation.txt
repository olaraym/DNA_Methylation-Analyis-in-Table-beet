# Performance evaluation of the methylation callers (Primrose/Pb_CpG and CCS_meth)
# In order to comprehensively evaluate the performance of 5mC predictions from the two pipelines utilized in this study on the PacBio Hifi reads, 
# I down-sampled the called methylation to at least 4X coverage based on the two sequencing technologies. Then I further filtered the detected 
# cytosine methylated positions into three categories which are two representing fully methylated (consisting of positions with probabilities of ≥ 99% and ≥ 50 respectively) 
# while the other category was for fully unmethylated (consisting of positions with probabilities of ≤ 1%). Furthermore, I devised a meticulous performance evaluation framework 
# consisting of four performance metrics (Precision, Recall, F1-score, and Accuracy). Having conducted an in-depth analysis of the long-read sequencing dataset using two approaches, 
# I compared the methylated CpG sites identified from these data using the methylated CpG data obtained from EM_Seq data. In this comparison, I assessed the precision, accuracy, recall, 
# and F1 score as the performance metrics. 

The methylated CG site data from the EM_Seq data obtained from the Bsbolt pipeline was used as a truth set to benchmark the performance of the two Pacbio Hifi read methylation 
detection pipelines used in this project. Based on the EM_seq data used as a truth set, a CpG position is defined to be fully methylated with high confidence if the methylation 
probability is ≥ 99% with a coverage of at least four (4) per read for 5mC probability prediction. However, because of the genome coverage of the Hifi reads used in this research, 
I set another methylation confidence at a methylation probability of ≥ 50% and a per-read coverage of ≥ 4.  
In a similar manner, I defined a CpG position as unmethylated if the coverage was  ≥ 4 and the corresponding CpG locus had a methylation probability of ≤ 1%. To calculate these metrics, 
I employed the following formula:

• Precision: The ratio of true positive predictions to the total predicted positives, indicating the proportion of correctly predicted 5mC sites.
Precision = TP / (TP+FP)
• Accuracy: The ratio of true positive and true negative predictions to the total number of predictions, providing an overall measure of the correctness of the predictions.
Accuracy = (TP + TN) / (TP + TN + FP + FN)
• Recall: The ratio of true positive predictions to the total actual positive sites, reflecting the ability of the pipeline to identify all relevant 5mC sites.
Recall = TP / (TP + FN)
•	F1 score: The harmonic mean of precision and recall, offering a balanced assessment of the overall performance by considering both precision and recall.
F1 score = 2 × (Recall × Precision) / (Recall + Precision).
